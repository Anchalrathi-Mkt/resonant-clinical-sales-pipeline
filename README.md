# Resonant Clinical | B2B Sales Prospecting & CRM Dashboard

## Project Overview
A portfolio project demonstrating B2B sales prospecting, lead qualification, CRM pipeline management, and business intelligence reporting for the life sciences industry.

## Key Features
- 30 potential B2B client companies
- Pharmaceutical, biotechnology, CRO, and diagnostic industry segmentation
- Lead qualification and prospect research
- Potential business needs and service matching
- Sales opportunity tracking
- Follow-up management
- Excel dashboard with Power BI-inspired visual design

## Tools & Skills
Microsoft Excel | CRM Management | B2B Prospecting | Sales Analytics | Market Research | Data Visualization

## Business Objective
To demonstrate how structured prospect research, accurate CRM records, and reporting can help commercial development teams identify and manage potential sales opportunities.

## Disclaimer
This is an independent educational portfolio project, not an official Resonant Clinical Solutions project. Potential opportunities are research hypotheses and do not represent confirmed customer relationships or sales activity.
